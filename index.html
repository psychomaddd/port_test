How I Lead Four things I actually believe
01
Build the practice, not just the product

Design without systems is just luck. I've built DesignOps from zero because great designers in a broken system can't do great work. Infrastructure has to come first.

02
Shape the brief, not just execute it

Design's best work starts before the brief exists. I push my team into product conversations early — to shape what gets built, not just how it looks after someone else has decided.

03
Growing people is the real job

I measure my success by what my designers do next. A designer who's better in 6 months than today is the highest-ROI thing I can do. I track careers, not just sprints.

04
Outcomes, not output

I don't celebrate what shipped. I celebrate what changed — for users, for the business, and for how the team works next time. Delivery is table stakes. Impact is the goal.
